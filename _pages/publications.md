## 1. Plot-and-Ask: Multimodal Local LLMs in Smart Environments for Visual IoT Analytics

**Authors:** A. Varol, A. Shaikh, N.H. Motlagh, M. Leino, J. Virkki  
**Conference:** 2026 21st Conference on Computer Science and Intelligence Systems (FedCSIS), ACSIS Vol. 47  
**Pages:** 609-614  
**Year:** 2026  
**Summary:** This paper presents a local multimodal analytics architecture for smart indoor environments that combines edge-resident multimodal large language models (MLLMs) with a knowledge graph (KG) policy layer. The proposed plot-and-ask workflow turns longer sensing periods into time-series plots with KG-derived comfort bands and passes them to a local vision-enabled model for interpretation, giving small models a compact representation of the data within their limited context windows. In a two-week indoor air quality deployment, all four evaluated local models achieved 100% accuracy for natural-language-to-SQL synthesis and plot generation, and Ministral 3 3B reached 100% plot-interpretation accuracy with 5.84 s end-to-end latency, with all processing kept on local hardware.  
**Link:** [View Publication](https://doi.org/10.15439/2026F4180)  
**Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/AygunVarol/multimodal-local)

## 2. Multi-Agent Large Language Models for Distributed Internet of Things Analytics in Smart Environments

**Authors:** A. Varol, A. Shaikh, N.H. Motlagh, M. Leino, J. Virkki  
**Conference:** 2026 11th International Conference on Smart and Sustainable Technologies (SpliTech)  
**Year:** 2026  
**Summary:** This paper presents a hierarchical multi-agent architecture in which a supervisor agent on an edge device delegates IoT analytics tasks to location-specific worker agents running on Raspberry Pi devices, with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) grounding the outputs in sensor measurements. Deployed with BME680 sensors in an office, a kitchen, and a hallway at Tampere University, the system achieves 100% accuracy across 135 queries under normal operation, retains 88.9% with one agent offline, and drops to 62.2% when a single compromised agent returns fabricated values, highlighting trust-aware aggregation as a key design requirement for multi-agent smart environments.  
**Link:** [View Publication](https://doi.org/10.23919/SpliTech70133.2026.11685466)

## 3. Evil-AI Benchmark: An Evaluation Framework for Adversarial Security Assessment of LLM Agents in Smart Environments

**Authors:** A. Varol, A. Shaikh, H. Jalo, G. Muchenje, J.S. de Cerqueira, K.-K. Kemell, N.H. Motlagh, M. Leino, H. Pirkkalainen, P. Abrahamsson, J. Virkki  
**Journal:** IEEE Access, Vol. 14  
**Pages:** 133098-133116  
**Year:** 2026  
**Summary:** This paper introduces Evil-AI Benchmark, an open-source framework for assessing adversarial threats to LLM agents in smart environments. It evaluates five threat vectors—prompt injection, persuasion, attacker-in-the-middle simulation, data leakage, and unsafe actions—alongside device-control capability and over-refusal, including physical verification of actuator commands. Eight LLMs are tested using 350 scenarios, including 250 adversarial tests. Observed attack success rates range from 0.8% to 54.0%, with persuasion and prompt injection presenting persistent weaknesses. The benchmark provides reproducible safety metrics for cyber-physical LLM applications.  
**Link:** [View Publication](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3727159)

## 4. Measuring the Atmosphere: Environmental Sensing for Occupant Flows in Commercial Spaces

**Authors:** N.H. Motlagh, A. Varol, M.A. Zaidan, P.L. Fung, S. Varjonen, P. Nurmi, S. Tarkoma  
**Journal:** IEEE Internet of Things Journal  
**Year:** 2026  
**Summary:** This paper investigates privacy-preserving visitor-flow estimation using environmental sensors deployed across a commercial building. Fourteen multivariable sensor nodes collect data in a shopping mall for up to 79 days, with ground-truth visitor counts available at one location. A compact multimodal attention-based model fuses environmental signals to classify low, medium, and high occupancy, achieving 76.5% balanced accuracy under a temporally blocked data split. The model outperforms seven baselines while using only 5,813 parameters (approximately 23 kB), demonstrating the potential for resource-constrained edge inference.  
**Link:** [View Publication](https://doi.org/10.1109/JIOT.2026.3738935)

## 8. Performance of Large Language Models Across Edge and Cloud Platforms in Smart Spaces

**Authors:** A. Varol, N.H. Motlagh, M. Leino, J. Virkki  
**Conference:** 2025 10th International Conference on Smart and Sustainable Technologies (SpliTech)  
**Year:** 2025  
**Summary:** This paper presents a hybrid edge–cloud framework for deploying large language models (LLMs) in smart spaces, integrating open-source models on edge devices (laptops, Raspberry Pis) with a commercial cloud platform. It evaluates performance in terms of latency and accuracy across descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics using both historical and real-time environmental data. Findings highlight the complementary strengths of cloud-based and edge-based deployments, with a task-aware split improving performance, privacy, and resilience in AI-driven smart environments.  
**Link:** [View Publication](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11091771)

## 9. From Prompts to Motors: Man-in-the-Middle Attacks on LLM-Enabled Vacuum Robots

**Authors:** A. Shaikh, A. Varol, J. Virkki  
**Journal:** IEEE Access, Vol. 13  
**Pages:** 137505-137513  
**Year:** 2025  
**Summary:** This paper presents a three-phase testbed and gray-box threat model for man-in-the-middle (MITM) attacks on an LLM-enabled vacuum robot. The presented system converts natural-language prompts into motor commands. The study evaluates four attacks—two indirect prompt-injection and two output-manipulation scenarios—across GPT-4, GPT-4o mini, and GPT-3.5 Turbo in simulated, tabletop, and physical trials, showing that a remote adversary who intercepts JSON messages can bypass collision avoidance, override motor control, and deliver deceptive feedback to users.  
**Link:** [View Publication](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11108294)

### Enabling Cloud-Level Accuracy in Edge AI through IoT Data Preprocessing

**Authors:** A. Varol, K. Kołodziej, Ł. Sobczak, M. Romaszewski, P. Głomb, N.H. Motlagh, M. Leino, J. Virkki  
**Preprint:** arXiv:2606.22496  
**Year:** 2026  
**Summary:** This paper examines whether prompt preprocessing can help small local LLMs interpret IoT air-quality and thermal-comfort data nearly as well as cloud models. Raw sensor readings are converted into progressively richer text, from raw values to threshold-aware descriptions and summary flags, and five local and five cloud LLMs are evaluated on indoor and outdoor datasets with and without chain-of-thought prompting. Prompt enrichment substantially improves local-model accuracy, showing that lightweight preprocessing can narrow the gap between local and cloud performance.  
**Link:** [View Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22496)
